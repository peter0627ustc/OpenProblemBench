# ORB-PHYS-80: Fermionic sum sides for $\mathcal{W}_r$ minimal-model characters and $r$-cylindric partition generating functions at ranks $r\geq 4$

The generating functions of $r$-cylindric partitions with infinitely long rows are, up to a factor $(q;q)_\infty$, the normalized characters of the $\mathcal{W}_r$ minimal models $\mathcal{M}_r^{r,r+d}$, and their product sides are known for every rank through Borodin's hook-length formula. Their fermionic sum sides — nested $q$-series sums with manifestly non-negative coefficients, i.e. the sum sides of the higher-rank Andrews–Gordon–Bressoud (Rogers–Ramanujan-type) identities — are known only for $r=2$ (Andrews–Gordon–Bressoud) and $r=3$ (Andrews–Schilling–Warnaar; Feigin–Foda–Welsh). Foda and Welsh stated in 2016 that "the $\mathcal{W}_r$ sum sides, for $r=4,5,\cdots$, are yet to be found", and this audit finds the problem open: a systematic forward-citation and keyword sweep shows that all subsequent progress on cylindric sum sides concerns rank 2, rank 3 (still partly conjectural), or the infinite-level limit, while Warnaar (2023) explicitly restates the arbitrary-rank $A_{r-1}^{(1)}$ Andrews–Gordon identities as an important open problem. The audited problem asks for explicit fermionic multi-sum expressions for the $r$-cylindric partition generating functions / $\mathcal{W}_r$ minimal-model characters at every rank $r\ge4$, together with proofs of the resulting sum–product identities against Borodin's product formula.

## Background

The Rogers–Ramanujan identities and their Andrews–Gordon and Bressoud generalizations equate a product side with a sum side that is a nested multiple sum with manifestly non-negative coefficients. Such identities have two readings. In representation theory, the sum sides are fermionic character formulae: characters of Virasoro minimal models (two-dimensional conformal field theories with finitely many irreducible representations, labelled by coprime integers $p<p'$) written as quasi-particle sums, as opposed to the alternating-sign ("bosonic") series produced by free-boson oscillator sums. In statistical mechanics, both sides arise as generating functions of one-dimensional lattice paths that describe local order parameters of exactly solved solid-on-solid lattice models (two-dimensional models whose local degrees of freedom are integer heights constrained by interaction rules around each face) computed by corner transfer matrices (a technique reducing one-point functions of two-dimensional models to one-dimensional path sums); the Andrews–Baxter–Forrester eight-vertex solid-on-solid model is the prototype, and fermionic forms underlie the thermodynamic Bethe ansatz (a finite-temperature method that derives integral equations for a model from its exact scattering data).

The $\mathcal{W}_r$ algebras are chiral (holomorphic-sector) symmetry algebras with $r-1$ generating currents of spins $2,3,\ldots,r$ — the Virasoro algebra is the case $r=2$ — and their minimal models $\mathcal{M}_r^{p,p'}$ are the higher-rank counterparts of the Virasoro minimal models. Foda and Welsh (2016) connected the characters of $\mathcal{M}_r^{r,r+d}$ to cylindric partitions: plane partitions (finite arrays of non-negative integers, weakly decreasing along rows and down columns) with $r$ rows that additionally obey the cylindric condition $\pi_{r,j}\ge\pi_{1,j+d}$, so that the array can be wrapped on a cylinder of circumference $r+d$; the relative shifts of the rows define a profile. The generating function $Z^{(r)}_\Lambda(q)=\sum_\pi q^{|\pi|}$, summed over cylindric partitions with infinitely long rows and profile encoded by a level-$d$ dominant integral weight $\Lambda=\sum_i m_i\Lambda_i$ (non-negative integers $m_i$ with $\sum_i m_i=d$) of the affine Kac–Moody algebra $\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_r=A^{(1)}_{r-1}$ ("level" being the weight's value on the canonical central element), satisfies

$$
Z^{(r)}_\Lambda(q)=\frac{\mathrm{Pr}\,\chi^{\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_r}_\Lambda}{(q^r;q^r)_\infty}=\frac{\chi^{\mathcal{W}_r}_\Lambda}{(q;q)_\infty},
$$

where $(a;q)_\infty=\prod_{n\ge0}(1-aq^n)$ is the $q$-Pochhammer symbol, $\mathrm{Pr}\,\chi$ is the principally specialized character (all specialization variables set equal to $q$), and $\chi^{\mathcal{W}_r}_\Lambda$ is the normalized character of the minimal model $\mathcal{M}_r^{r,r+d}$, defined when $\gcd(r,d)=1$. The product side is known for all $r$ by Borodin's formula

$$
Z^{(r)}_\Lambda(q)=\frac{1}{(q^{r+d};q^{r+d})_\infty}\prod_{\substack{1\le i<j\le r+d\\ \omega_i>\omega_j}}\frac{1}{(q^{j-i};q^{r+d})_\infty}\prod_{\substack{1\le i<j\le r+d\\ \omega_i<\omega_j}}\frac{1}{(q^{r+d-j+i};q^{r+d})_\infty},
$$

a hook-length-type product determined by the binary word $\omega=\omega(\Lambda)$ with $r$ zeros and $d$ ones that encodes the profile.

For $r=2$, Foda and Welsh re-derived the Andrews–Gordon and Bressoud identities by mapping 2-cylindric partitions through two-string abaci (bead-and-runner encodings of partitions) to decorated versions of Bressoud's restricted lattice paths, and transforming between paths obeying different restrictions so as to obtain sum expressions with manifestly non-negative coefficients; equating these with Borodin's product and cancelling the common factor $(q;q)_\infty^{-1}$ yields the identities. For $r=3$, fermionic sum sides are known from the $A_2$ Bailey lemma of Andrews, Schilling and Warnaar — whose Rogers–Ramanujan-type identities express principally specialized characters of level-$d$ standard $A^{(1)}_2$-modules as multiple sums — and from the construction of Feigin, Foda and Welsh out of combinations of Virasoro characters. For $r\ge4$ nothing comparable exists, and this is precisely the gap the source states.

## Problem Statement

Find explicit fermionic expressions — $q$-series given by nested multiple sums with manifestly non-negative coefficients — for the generating functions $Z^{(r)}_\Lambda(q)$ of $r$-cylindric partitions with infinitely long rows, at every rank $r\ge4$, where $\Lambda$ ranges over the dominant integral weights of $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$ of arbitrary level $d\ge1$ (equivalently, for the normalized characters $\chi^{\mathcal{W}_r}_\Lambda=(q;q)_\infty Z^{(r)}_\Lambda(q)$ of the minimal models $\mathcal{M}_r^{r,r+d}$, which are defined when $\gcd(r,d)=1$), and prove the resulting sum–product identities

$$
\text{fermionic sum side} = \text{Borodin's product side}
$$

for each admissible $\Lambda$. These are the rank-$r\ge4$ analogues of the Andrews–Gordon–Bressoud identities (the $r=2$ case) and of the Andrews–Schilling–Warnaar and Feigin–Foda–Welsh identities (the $r=3$ case); a sum side at rank $r$ is expected to involve at least $r-1$ nested summation variables carrying the $A_{r-1}$ structure. The method is unrestricted: the source's route through $r$-string abaci and Bressoud-type transforms is one proposed template, not a requirement. A general-rank solution would in particular subsume the currently conjectural manifestly positive formulas for cylindric partitions of rank 3 and level $d$ not divisible by 3.

The verification contract below evaluates answers to this statement. It does not narrow or redefine the research question.

Known solving difficulties:

- The rank-$r$ sum side must carry $A_{r-1}$ structure with at least $r-1$ nested summation variables; no existing identity exhibits this structure (the low-level identities obtainable from $A_1$ and $A_2$ by level-rank duality do not), and even the conjectural shape of the answer at $r\ge4$ is unknown, so the solver must first guess or derive the correct form.
- The $r=2$ proof rests on Bressoud's transforms between restricted one-dimensional lattice paths; at $r\ge3$ the analogous objects are walks on restricted $(r-1)$-dimensional lattices, and no transform calculus for them is known.
- No Bailey lemma or Bailey tree is available beyond $A_2$; the $A_2$ tree required substantial new machinery, and an $A_{r-1}$ ($r\ge4$) analogue would have to be developed from scratch.
- The Corteel–Welsh functional recursions for cylindric-partition generating functions are established at rank 3, but formulating and solving the corresponding systems at $r\ge4$ is open.
- Any proof must control the cancellation of the common factor $(q;q)_\infty^{-1}$ between the sum and product sides — a delicate point that was already central in the $r=2$ case — while keeping every coefficient manifestly non-negative.

## Current Progress

- Status: `ready`

Source and formulation check. Foda and Welsh (2016) state verbatim (footnote to their section 1.10, confirmed against the arXiv LaTeX source of 1510.02213): "the $\mathcal{W}_r$ product sides are known from the $r$-cylindric partition approach, for all $r$. The $\mathcal{W}_3$ sum sides are known from [Andrews–Schilling–Warnaar 1999, Feigin–Foda–Welsh 2008]. The $\mathcal{W}_r$ sum sides, for $r=4,5,\cdots$, are yet to be found. The purpose of this work is to pave a possible way to compute them." The paper realizes only the $r=2$ case (re-deriving the Andrews–Gordon–Bressoud identities through 2-string abaci and Bressoud-type path transforms), and its section 10.2 adds the softer remark that the methods "can be extended to investigate Rogers–Ramanujan-type identities for other affine Lie algebras", promising simpler $\mathcal{W}_3$ proofs elsewhere (later delivered by Corteel–Welsh and by Warnaar).

Correction of the candidate paraphrase. The LKM open-question node attributed to the paper three additional "unresolved tasks": extensions to non-coprime parameter regimes and to supersymmetric/fused extensions, and generalization to affine Lie algebras beyond $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$. Only the last appears in the paper, and only as a methodological remark, not a posed problem; the non-coprime/supersymmetric items are embellishments of a scope footnote (the paper notes it does not treat supersymmetric extensions where $p,p'$ need not be coprime). The faithful open core is exactly the $r\ge4$ sum sides; the audited problem is stated accordingly, and the adjacent-algebra direction is recorded here as context rather than folded into the problem.

Corteel and Welsh (Annals of Combinatorics 23, 2019) re-derived the Andrews–Schilling–Warnaar $A_2$ Rogers–Ramanujan identities directly from cylindric partitions and introduced functional recursions for cylindric-partition generating functions (the Corteel–Welsh recursions), which became the main technical engine for later rank-3 work. No rank-$r\ge4$ sum sides were produced.

Corteel, Dousse and Uncu (Proceedings of the AMS 150, 2022) computed the generating functions for cylindric partitions of all profiles $(c_1,c_2,c_3)$ with $c_1+c_2+c_3=5$ and discovered and proved seven new $A_2$ Rogers–Ramanujan identities modulo 8 with quadruple sums — again rank 3 only.

Warnaar (Transactions of the AMS, Series B 10, 2023) is the key status paper. He proves $A_2$ Andrews–Gordon identities for the cases $s\in\{1,k,k+1\}$ of his Conjecture 2.1 (other cases conditionally), proves $q$-series identities corresponding to the infinite-level limit of the Andrews–Gordon identities for $A_{r-1}$ for arbitrary rank $r$, and formulates still-open conjectural manifestly positive two-variable formulas for cylindric partitions of rank 3 and level $d$ not divisible by 3. His introduction states that "finding the higher-rank generalisation of the Andrews–Gordon for the seemingly simpler affine Lie algebra $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$ remains an open problem", and his section 8 states: "An important open problem is to find the $A_{r-1}$ (or $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$) Rogers–Ramanujan and Andrews–Gordon identities for arbitrary rank $r$", noting that a rank-$r$ sum side needs at least $r-1$ summation variables and that the low-level identities implied by level-rank duality (level 2 from $A_1$, level 3 from $A_2$) do not exhibit the full $A_{r-1}$ structure.

Warnaar (Journal of the European Mathematical Society, arXiv:2303.09069) constructed a four-parameter $A_2$ Bailey tree, proved the Kanade–Russell conjecture for a three-parameter family of Rogers–Ramanujan-type identities for principally specialized $A^{(1)}_2$ characters, and thereby obtained an $A^{(1)}_2$ analogue of the Andrews–Gordon identities — rank 3 only; no Bailey machinery exists for $A_{r-1}$ with $r\ge4$.

Kanade and Russell (International Mathematics Research Notices, 2023) proved complete sets of Andrews–Schilling–Warnaar-form identities for $A^{(1)}_2$ principal characters at moduli 5 through 8 and 10 (moduli 6 and 10 new), gave conjectures for modulus 9, and showed how "seed" conjectures truncated from the infinite level generate complete conjecture sets — rank 3 only. Uncu (arXiv:2301.01359) subsequently proved the modulo 11 and 13 cylindric Kanade–Russell conjectures, and Tsuchioka (Journal of the London Mathematical Society, arXiv:2205.04811) gave manifestly positive Andrews–Gordon-type series for the level-3 standard modules of $A^{(1)}_2$ — all rank 3.

Kanade (Letters in Mathematical Physics 113, 2023) showed that limits of $\mathfrak{sl}_r$-coloured torus-knot invariants are, up to $r$-dependent factors, the characters of the $\mathcal{W}_r(p,p')$ minimal models (and hence modular) — a new characterization valid for all ranks, but not a fermionic sum side.

Kanade and Russell (Proceedings of the AMS, arXiv:2508.15113, "Tight cylindric partitions") initiated the study of a restricted class of cylindric partitions, gave analogues of the Corteel–Welsh functional equations valid for general $r$-rowed tight cylindric partitions, but obtained closed forms only for 2-rowed ones; the rank-$r\ge4$ sum-side problem is untouched.

Adjacent directions related to the source's remark on other affine Lie algebras: Takigiku and Tsuchioka (arXiv:2606.31821) proved Andrews–Gordon-type sum–product identities for level-2 standard modules of $A^{(2)}_{\mathrm{odd}}$ (except $A^{(2)}_{6n+3}$), generalizing Göllnitz–Gordon, Rogers–Ramanujan and (partially) Nandi; Warnaar (arXiv:2511.17034) derived $q,t$-analogues of Rogers–Ramanujan identities for the $C^{(1)}_n$, $A^{(2)}_{2n}$ and $D^{(2)}_{n+2}$ GOW identities. None of this concerns the principal $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$/$\mathcal{W}_r$ family of the source problem.

Coverage and residual uncertainty. The audit performed a forward-citation sweep of the source paper (33 citing works via Semantic Scholar), a forward-citation sweep of Warnaar's 2021/2023 paper (7 citing works), and full arXiv keyword sweeps ("cylindric partitions": all 29 hits; "Andrews-Gordon": 60 hits; combinations of "W_4"/"W_r" with "fermionic": no relevant hits). All post-2015 sum-side progress is at ranks 2–3 or at infinite level; no work was found that derives, or even conjectures, finite-level fermionic sum sides for $r\ge4$. Remaining uncertainty: non-arXiv-indexed literature (books, proceedings) was checked only through citation chains, and very recent unpublished work would not be visible; the boundary between the univariate character sum side and the refined two-variable cylindric generating function also leaves the rank-3 conjectures of Warnaar open, which a general-rank solution would subsume.

## Scientific Significance

Affected-field significance: `medium`.

Solving the problem would complete the bosonic–fermionic (product–sum) duality program for $\mathcal{W}_r$ minimal-model characters and cylindric partitions: it would add the higher-rank Andrews–Gordon–Bressoud identities to the stock of fundamental $q$-series identities, provide quasi-particle (fermionic) bases for the characters of all principal $\mathcal{W}_r$ minimal models, and supply the sum sides needed for fermionic evaluations of order parameters in higher-rank exactly solved solid-on-solid models. The impact is direct within $q$-series and partition theory and the representation theory of W-algebras and affine $A^{(1)}_{r-1}$ — it would settle a gap explicitly flagged as an important open problem by Foda–Welsh (2016) and Warnaar (2023) — and indirect for the broader theory of exactly solved lattice models; it would not by itself change methods outside this circle.
