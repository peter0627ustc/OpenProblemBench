# ORB-PHYS-66: Explicit closed-form evaluation of the partial partition functions of the three-coloring model with domain wall boundary conditions in the general elliptic case

Razumov and Stroganov (2008) proved that the partial partition functions of the three-coloring statistical model with domain wall boundary conditions satisfy three-term theta-functional equations, and solved them in a nontrivial trigonometric limit, obtaining a determinant representation built from two alternating-power determinants with explicit scalar prefactors. They closed by posing, as a challenging open problem, the corresponding explicit expression for the general elliptic case (generic nome $p\neq0$). This audit verifies the source formulation against the primary paper and traces the subsequent literature: the total elliptic domain-wall partition function of the parent eight-vertex-solid-on-solid model is known (Rosengren's Izergin-Korepin-type identity; Galléas's single-determinant representations), and the homogeneous three-colour partition function is known through recursive polynomials and Hankel determinants, but the three $\mathbb{Z}_3$-color sectors individually have no closed form at generic elliptic parameters, and the constructive solution of the elliptic functional equations remains unavailable. The problem is audited as open, with a sharp surviving core.

## Background

The three-coloring model of Baxter (1970) is a lattice statistical model whose states are colorings of the faces of a square grid with three colors such that adjacent faces always carry different colors; Baxter computed its free energy on the torus by relating it to the zero-field eight-vertex model. Razumov and Stroganov (2008-2009) initiated the study of this model under domain wall boundary conditions (DWBC), the boundary setup in which the six-vertex model is exactly solved by the Izergin-Korepin determinant (a closed determinant formula for the inhomogeneous six-vertex model partition function on an $n\times n$ square with all boundary arrows pointing inward). For the three-coloring model, DWBC means that walking anticlockwise along the boundary the color changes by $+1$ from face to face along the vertical sides and by $-1$ along the horizontal sides; the admissible states of an $(n+1)\times(n+1)$ grid of faces are in bijection with alternating sign matrices (square matrices with entries $0,\pm1$ whose nonzero entries alternate in sign and sum to one in each row and column). With Stroganov's integrable weights, the Boltzmann weight of each of the six local vertex configurations is built from Jacobi theta functions of nome $p=e^{i\pi\tau}$ (with $\mathrm{Im}\,\tau>0$, so $p=0$ is the trigonometric limit), and depends on the spectral parameter $\chi_i-\psi_j$ attached to the crossing of horizontal line $i$ and vertical line $j$, on a fixed parameter $\lambda$, and on a color index $r\in\mathbb{Z}_3$. The total partition function decomposes over the residual $\mathbb{Z}_3$ symmetry as $Z_n=\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}_3}Z_n^r$, where the partial partition function $Z_n^r$ sums the states of one boundary-color sector. Part I of the series proved that the normalized functions $F_n^r$ (the sectors divided by explicit theta-function factors) satisfy three-term theta-functional equations such as $\sum_{s\in\mathbb{Z}_3}F_n^{r+s}(\chi_1,\ldots,\chi_k+2\pi s/3,\ldots,\chi_n;\{\psi\})=0$, together with the analogous equations in the $\psi$ variables. Part II solved these equations in a nontrivial trigonometric limit ($p\to0$ after the shift $\lambda\to\lambda+\pi\tau/2$): after a discrete Fourier transform in the sector index, the transformants are proportional to two alternating-power determinants $\mathcal{P}_n$ and $\mathcal{Q}_n$ in the $2n$ interlaced variables $u_1,\ldots,u_{2n}$, and each partial partition function is an explicit linear combination of $\mathcal{P}_n$ and $\mathcal{Q}_n$ with scalar prefactors $A_n^r,B_n^r,C_n^r$ fixed by recursion relations. In the naive trigonometric limit each sector $Z_n^r$ separately coincides with the six-vertex DWBC partition function at crossing parameter $2\pi/3$, that is, with the Izergin-Korepin determinant at a third root of unity (Stroganov). The model is a special, root-of-unity-anisotropy case of Baxter's eight-vertex-solid-on-solid (8VSOS) model, an ice-type model whose elliptic weights satisfy the dynamical Yang-Baxter equation (a Yang-Baxter equation with an extra shift parameter, the dynamical parameter $\lambda$); Rosengren obtained an Izergin-Korepin-type identity, a sum of Frobenius-type determinants, for the total 8VSOS DWBC partition function at generic elliptic parameters, and Galléas later produced continuous families of single-determinant representations of that total partition function. All of these results concern the total partition function; they do not resolve the three color sectors individually, and no elliptic analogue of the $\mathcal{P}_n/\mathcal{Q}_n$ sector formulas is known.

## Problem Statement

Find an explicit closed-form expression for the partial partition functions $Z_n^r(\{\chi\};\{\psi\})$ (equivalently, for the normalized functions $F_n^r$) of the three-coloring statistical model with domain wall boundary conditions and Stroganov's elliptic vertex weights, in the general elliptic case of generic nome $p\neq0$, generic fixed parameter $\lambda$, and generic inhomogeneous spectral parameters $\chi_i,\psi_j$; this is the problem posed in the closing sentence of Razumov and Stroganov, Part II. The expression is the one 'corresponding' to the trigonometric result in the following determinate sense: (a) it must be given for every lattice size $n$ and every sector $r\in\mathbb{Z}_3$ individually, with the dependence on $\lambda$ and the nome $p$ explicit; (b) in the degeneration $p\to0$ (after the substitution $\lambda\to\lambda+\pi\tau/2$) it must reduce to the trigonometric representation of Part II, that is, to linear combinations of the determinants $\mathcal{P}_n$ and $\mathcal{Q}_n$ with the scalar prefactors $A_n^r,B_n^r,C_n^r$; and (c) in the naive trigonometric limit each sector must reproduce the Izergin-Korepin determinant at a third root of unity. An equivalent and equally acceptable route is a constructive solution of the elliptic three-term theta-functional equations satisfied by $F_n^r$ (Part I), from which such closed forms are extracted. Determinants, pfaffians, elliptic hypergeometric sums, or any other explicit special-function form are admissible, provided each color sector is obtained individually and the sum $\sum_{r\in\mathbb{Z}_3}Z_n^r$ is consistent with the known 8VSOS domain-wall partition function identities.

The verification contract below evaluates answers to this statement. It does not narrow or redefine the research question.

Known solving difficulties:

- The trigonometric solution pins its answer by counting polynomial degrees after a discrete Fourier transform; in the elliptic regime the natural objects are elliptic polynomials (higher-order theta functions), whose solution spaces for the three-term relation are larger, so the dimension bookkeeping that forces proportionality to a single determinant does not carry over and new structure or uniqueness lemmas must be established.
- The natural candidate objects, elliptic deformations of the alternating-power (Schur/Okada-type) determinants $\mathcal{P}_n$ and $\mathcal{Q}_n$, elliptic pfaffians, or elliptic hypergeometric determinants, require proving new multivariate elliptic identities with a prescribed pole structure in all $2n$ spectral variables; existing elliptic determinant evaluations may not match that structure.
- The scalar prefactors $A_n^r,B_n^r,C_n^r$ must be promoted to elliptic functions of $\lambda$ and $p$; fixing them uses the elliptic specialization recursions, which involve the gauge-transformed ('tilde') sector partition functions of Part I and their separate symmetry properties, adding a layer absent from the trigonometric computation.
- The answer may not be a single determinant or a small combination of them: for the related homogeneous three-colour model Rosengren reported 'no very simple formula', and the sector structure may force sums of several determinant or pfaffian terms, enlarging the space of candidate ansätze.
- Degeneration constraints cut in two directions: the expression must reproduce both trigonometric limits (the naive limit giving the third-root-of-unity Izergin-Korepin determinant per sector, and the nontrivial limit of Part II), which strongly constrains but also complicates any ansatz.

## Current Progress

- Status: `ready`

Source fidelity: the full text of arXiv:0812.2654 was inspected. The paper states the open problem in a single closing sentence: 'Thus, we found an explicit expression for the partial partition functions of the three-coloring statistical model in the trigonometric limit. The challenging problem is to find the corresponding expression for the general elliptic case.' The LKM record (node gcn_9c284577f31f455e) is faithful in this core, but its enumerated sub-items (constructive solution of the theta-function three-term relations; whether the elliptic sectors are elliptic deformations of the $\mathcal{P}_n$ and $\mathcal{Q}_n$ determinants; explicit elliptic analogues of the prefactors $A_n^r,B_n^r,C_n^r$) and its phrase about deriving Rosengren-type elliptic identities are elaborations not stated verbatim in the paper; the Rosengren discussion appears only as motivation in the introduction ('we use the functional equations and hope that the present consideration will be useful for consideration of the general elliptic case'). The formulation here is corrected to the primary source: the load-bearing target is the closed-form elliptic evaluation of the partial partition functions, with the functional-equation route recorded as the equivalent constructive formulation.

Rosengren (arXiv:0801.1229, Adv. Appl. Math. 43 (2009) 137-155) obtained an Izergin-Korepin-type identity for the total DWBC partition function of the 8VSOS model at generic elliptic parameters, as a sum of Frobenius-type determinants. Razumov and Stroganov themselves note that their trigonometric sector representation could in principle be extracted from this identity using Okada's determinant results; no analogous extraction of the three color sectors at generic nome $p$ has been carried out, and the total partition function does not determine the sectors individually.

Rosengren (arXiv:0911.0561, Adv. Appl. Math. 46 (2011) 481-535) studied the homogeneous three-colour model with DWBC (Baxter's colour-weight parametrization) and expressed its partition function through recursively constructed special polynomials $p_n$, remarking that 'there seems to be no very simple formula for the general three-colour partition function'; Bazhanov and Mangazeev subsequently conjectured faster recursions, and the polynomials were later tied to Painlevé VI tau functions and to the supersymmetric eight-vertex chain (Rosengren's special-polynomials series; Hagendorf-Rosengren 2022). This line concerns the homogeneous total partition function, not the inhomogeneous sectors.

Rosengren (arXiv:1605.02915, J. Stat. Mech. 2016) introduced multivariable elliptic pfaffians and showed that the 8VSOS DWBC partition function at the root-of-unity anisotropy equals a sum of two elliptic pfaffians, whose homogeneous limit expresses the three-colour DWBC partition function as a sum of two Hankel determinants. Again this is the total partition function; the partial partition functions are not addressed.

Galléas (arXiv:1207.5283, Nucl. Phys. B 867 (2013) 855-871) derived refined functional relations for the elliptic SOS model with DWBC and solved them as multiple contour integrals; he explicitly remarks that a possible connection between his functional relations and the Razumov-Stroganov functional equations for the three-colouring model 'has eluded us so far' (2013), which is direct evidence that the elliptic functional-equation program had not absorbed the Razumov-Stroganov problem.

Galléas (Phys. Rev. E 94 (2016) 010102; J. Math. Phys. 60 (2019) 023503; arXiv:1812.10781) obtained a single determinant, continuous families of single determinants, and further new determinantal representations for the total elliptic SOS DWBC partition function at generic anisotropy, all derived from functional equations. Full-text inspection of these papers shows no mention of the Razumov-Stroganov partial partition functions, the three-colouring specialization, or any claim to have solved the elliptic sector problem; Lamers's SIGMA 14 (2018) 064 review of the functional method for domain-wall partition functions likewise does not treat the Razumov-Stroganov equations.

No Part III of the Razumov-Stroganov series exists: the arXiv record of A. V. Razumov's subsequent work shows a move to quantum groups and qKZ equations after 2009, and the elliptic case of the three-coloring model was never revisited by the authors.

Coverage: citation sweeps via the Semantic Scholar graphs of both parts of the series and of the Rosengren and Galléas clusters (roughly thirty citing works spanning 2009-2022, including combinatorics follow-ups on alternating sign matrices, the homogeneous-polynomial/Painlevé thread, and supersymmetric eight-vertex work) found no claimed closed-form evaluation of the elliptic partial partition functions and no constructive solution of the elliptic three-term equations. INSPIRE does not index these math-ph papers, the OpenAlex quota was exhausted, and the web-search tool returned empty results (retrieval fell back to direct arXiv, Crossref, and Semantic Scholar API calls), so very recent (2023-2026) literature is covered only through the citation graphs; the complete silence across three tightly linked research clusters through 2022 and the absence of a Part III make a missed resolution unlikely but not impossible.

What remains open is precise and nonempty: explicit closed-form expressions for the three sectors $Z_n^r$ (equivalently $F_n^r$) at generic nome $p\neq0$, generic $\lambda$, and generic inhomogeneous spectral parameters, or equivalently a constructive solution of the theta-functional three-term equations of Part I in the elliptic regime. The known total-partition-function formulas (Rosengren, Galléas) do not determine the sectors, and the trigonometric proof technique, which pins the answer by counting polynomial degrees, has no established elliptic analogue since elliptic polynomials (higher-order theta functions) have larger solution spaces for the three-term relation.

An observation kept separate from the literature status: because the total elliptic partition function is known while each sector equals the Izergin-Korepin determinant at a third root of unity in the naive trigonometric limit, a natural attack would combine Rosengren's 8VSOS identity with elliptic Okada-type determinant manipulations; no such derivation appears in the retrieved literature, and this observation is not counted as any progress toward closure.

## Scientific Significance

Affected-field significance: `medium`.

Solving the problem would complete the Razumov-Stroganov exact-solution program for the three-coloring model with domain wall boundary conditions, extending the root-of-unity Izergin-Korepin determinant story (which underlies alternating-sign-matrix enumeration) from the trigonometric to the genuinely elliptic, dynamical regime. The direct impact is on exactly solvable lattice models and elliptic hypergeometric special functions: any closed form would necessarily embody new multivariate elliptic determinant or pfaffian identities, and through the bijection between domain-wall three-colorings and alternating sign matrices it would yield refined 'dynamical' enumerations in the elliptic regime. The impact is direct but confined to this specialist community and its follow-up threads (8VSOS functional relations, Painlevé VI tau functions), hence medium rather than high.
