# ORB-MATH-30: Parametrization of the volume conjecture for hyperbolic knots beyond the figure-eight knot

Murakami's parametrization of the volume conjecture (J. Topology, 2013; restated as Conjecture 1.6 of arXiv:2312.00350) predicts that for every hyperbolic knot $K$ the colored Jones polynomial $J_N(K;e^{(2\pi i+u)/N})$ has, for $u$ in a punctured neighborhood of $0$, a full leading asymptotic as $N\to\infty$ whose exponential growth is governed by the potential function $S_K(u)$ — recovering the $\mathrm{PSL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ Chern–Simons invariant of the deformed representation $\rho_u$ — and whose prefactor is the homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion $T_K(\rho_u)$. All rigorous verifications to date concern the figure-eight knot only: most recently for real $u>\operatorname{arccosh}(3/2)$ (arXiv:2312.00350, where $\rho_u$ is $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{R})$-valued) and for complex $\xi$ with $0<\operatorname{Im}\xi<\pi/2$ (arXiv:2602.01049, Feb 2026). The problem audited here is to prove or refute the conjecture for hyperbolic knots other than the figure-eight knot, up to full generality, with the sign convention of the adjoint Reidemeister torsion fixed — a point the source itself leaves unresolved even for the figure-eight knot.

## Background

The colored Jones polynomial $J_N(K;q)$ of a knot $K\subset S^3$ is the sequence of quantum invariants obtained by evaluating the knot with the $N$-dimensional irreducible representation of the quantum group $U_q(\mathfrak{sl}_2)$; $J_2$ is the classical Jones polynomial and $q$ is a complex parameter. Kashaev (1997) introduced an invariant built from the quantum dilogarithm and conjectured that it grows exponentially in $N$ at a rate given by the hyperbolic volume of the knot complement; Murakami and Murakami (2001) identified Kashaev's invariant with $J_N(K;e^{2\pi i/N})$ and formulated the Volume Conjecture: for any knot $K$,
$$
\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{2\pi}{N}\log\left|J_N\left(K;e^{2\pi i/N}\right)\right|=\operatorname{Vol}(S^3\setminus K),
$$
where $\operatorname{Vol}$ is the simplicial volume. A knot is hyperbolic when its complement carries a complete hyperbolic metric of finite volume; by Mostow rigidity this metric is unique, so the hyperbolic volume of the complement is a topological invariant, and for hyperbolic $K$ it coincides with the simplicial volume on the right-hand side. Dropping the absolute value gives a complex limit, conjectured by Murakami–Murakami–Okamoto–Takata–Yokota (2002) to equal the complex volume $\operatorname{Vol}(K)+i\,\mathrm{CS}^{\mathrm{SO}(3)}(K)$, where the Chern–Simons invariant is the secondary invariant of the Levi-Civita connection of the hyperbolic structure (the integral of the Chern–Simons 3-form over the complement, defined modulo $4\pi^2$). A finer refinement, developed by Gukov, by Dimofte–Gukov–Lenells–Zagier, and by Gukov–Murakami, predicts the subleading prefactor of the asymptotics in terms of the adjoint Reidemeister torsion $T_K(\rho)$ — the Whitehead torsion of the cochain complex of $S^3\setminus K$ with coefficients twisted by $\operatorname{Ad}\circ\rho$ for a flat $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ connection $\rho$, a classical secondary invariant measuring the homological size of the flat connection.

Murakami (Journal of Topology, 2013) proposed the parametrized version audited here: one perturbs the evaluation point $2\pi i$ by a complex number $u$ and studies $J_N(K;e^{(2\pi i+u)/N})$. Let $(\mu,\lambda)$ be the standard meridian/longitude pair of $K$, and let $\rho_u:\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\to\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ be the irreducible representation — deforming the holonomy representation for small $|u|$ — under which the meridian has eigenvalues $e^{\pm u/2}$ and the longitude has eigenvalues $-e^{\pm v(u)/2}$ for a companion function $v(u)$. The conjecture predicts a complete leading asymptotic combining three classical ingredients: a potential function $S_K(u)$ (a Neumann–Zagier-type potential encoding the complex volume of the deformed structure), the homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion $T_K(\rho_u)$ in Porti's normalization with the meridian as homology basis, and the $\mathrm{PSL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ Chern–Simons invariant; the precise formula is stated in the problem statement. Physically this is the predicted semiclassical asymptotics of $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ complex Chern–Simons theory, of which the colored Jones polynomial (the $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ theory) at the perturbed coupling is expected to be a sector.

The conjecture is proved only for the figure-eight knot $4_1$, in the regimes examined so far: the logarithmic limit for small $|u|$ (Murakami–Yokota 2007); the full formula for small $|u|$ with nonzero winding $p$, i.e. evaluation at $\xi=u+2p\pi i$ (Murakami 2013); the boundary value $u=\kappa=\operatorname{arccosh}(3/2)$, where the representation degenerates to the parabolic one (Murakami, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 2025; polynomial growth at $p=0$ was known from Hikami–Murakami 2008); real $u>\kappa$ with $p=0$ (Murakami–Tran 2025) and with integer $p\ge 1$ (Murakami, arXiv:2312.00350, the audited paper, where $\rho_u$ is $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{R})$-valued and the formula acquires an additional multiplicative factor $J_p(\mathscr{E};e^{4N\pi^2/\xi})$); the unperturbed point $\xi=2\pi i$ (Andersen–Hansen 2006); and complex $\xi$ with $0<\operatorname{Im}\xi<\pi/2$ (Murakami, arXiv:2602.01049). For hyperbolic knots other than $4_1$ only the single-point refinement at the unperturbed evaluation $\xi=2\pi i$ (torsion and Chern–Simons at that one point) is known, for knots with at most seven crossings (Andersen–Hansen for $4_1$; Ohtsuki for $5_2$ and the seven-crossing knots; Ohtsuki–Yokota for $6_1,6_2,6_3$), plus the ordinary volume conjecture (leading exponential rate only, no torsion or Chern–Simons prefactor) for some special families such as torus knots, twist knots, and double twist knots. Garoufalidis and Lê (2011) proved that for any knot $J_N(K;e^{u/N})\to 1/\Delta(K;e^u)$ for $u$ near $0$, where $\Delta$ is the Alexander polynomial, so the exponential growth concentrates on the $2\pi i$-sheets that the conjecture addresses. No verification of the parametrized conjecture — the full formula with torsion and Chern–Simons as functions of the deformation parameter $u$ — exists for any hyperbolic knot other than $4_1$.

## Problem Statement

Prove or refute the parametrization of the volume conjecture for hyperbolic knots other than the figure-eight knot. The conjecture (Murakami, J. Topology 6 (2013), 193–216; restated as Conjecture 1.6 of arXiv:2312.00350) asserts: for a hyperbolic knot $K$, there exists a neighborhood $U$ of $0$ in $\mathbb{C}$ such that for $u\in U\setminus\pi i\mathbb{Q}$,
$$
J_N\left(K;e^{(2\pi i+u)/N}\right)\ \underset{N\to\infty}{\sim}\ \frac{\sqrt{-\pi}}{2\sinh(u/2)}\,T_K(\rho_u)^{-1/2}\left(\frac{N}{2\pi i+u}\right)^{1/2}\exp\left(\frac{S_K(u)}{2\pi i+u}\,N\right),
$$
where $\rho_u$ is the irreducible $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ representation of $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$ with meridian eigenvalues $e^{\pm u/2}$, deforming the holonomy representation for small $|u|$; $T_K(\rho_u)$ is the homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion in Porti's normalization (meridian as homology generator); $S_K(u)$ is the potential function, from which the Chern–Simons invariant is recovered as $\mathrm{CS}_{u,v(u)}(\rho_u)=S_K(u)-u\pi i-u\,v(u)/4$ with $v(u)=2\,\frac{d}{du}S_K(u)-2\pi i$.

Since the figure-eight knot case is settled in the regimes listed in the background, the open content is every other hyperbolic knot, up to and including a proof for all hyperbolic knots. Any resolution must in particular fix the sign convention under which $T_K(\rho_u)$ enters the formula: the homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion is defined only up to an overall sign, and even the figure-eight identification in arXiv:2312.00350 is stated only 'up to a sign' (following Porti). A resolution by counterexample must exhibit a specific hyperbolic knot $K$ and an admissible parameter $u$ (with $\rho_u$ irreducible and $\mu$-regular) for which the asymptotic formula provably fails, or show that the required neighborhood $U$ cannot exist.

The verification contract below evaluates answers to this statement. It does not narrow or redefine the research question.

Known solving difficulties:

- The figure-eight proofs rest on an explicit quantum-dilogarithm summation formula for $J_N(4_1)$ reflecting the one-ideal-tetrahedron structure of its complement; for a general hyperbolic knot no comparable closed summation is available, so the asymptotic machinery itself (e.g., asymptotic solution of the $A$-polynomial recurrence, or state-integral/perturbative-series methods) must be developed from scratch.
- Uniform saddle-point and steepest-descent control is needed in both $N$ and the deformation parameter $u$, with branch choices of the potential function $S_K(u)$ and the dilogarithm tied to the knot's deformation variety; the required neighborhood $U$ of $0$ and the excluded countable set $\pi i\mathbb{Q}$ impose uniformity constraints up to accumulating singular parameters.
- The torsion prefactor requires identifying the Hessian-type determinant produced by the saddle-point analysis with Porti's homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion for a general irreducible $\rho_u$, including fixing the overall sign — a normalization left unresolved even in the figure-eight case.
- The Chern–Simons invariant of non-holonomy representations $\rho_u$ must be consistently normalized (lifting $\mathrm{PSL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ to $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ and choosing branches) so that it specializes to $i\operatorname{Vol}(K)-\mathrm{CS}^{\mathrm{SO}(3)}(K)$ at the holonomy.
- The statement quantifies over all hyperbolic knots, so a general proof must cope with arbitrarily complicated deformation varieties — collisions of representations, real components, and reducibles — whose geometry is far less controlled than for the figure-eight knot; known small-knot results suggest the combinatorial difficulty grows rapidly with crossing number.

## Current Progress

- Status: `ready`

Source fidelity verified against the arXiv TeX source of arXiv:2312.00350: Conjecture 1.6 there is exactly the Parametrization of the volume conjecture proposed by Murakami (J. Topology 6 (2013), 193–216), with antecedents in Gukov–Murakami (2008) and Dimofte–Gukov; the paper proves it for the figure-eight knot in the real regime $u>\kappa=\operatorname{arccosh}(3/2)$ with winding $p\ge 1$ (Theorem 1.11). The LKM paraphrase is faithful, not a conflation of adjacent conjectures: the source clearly separates the Volume Conjecture, its complexification, the torsion refinement at the unperturbed point, and the parametrized conjecture. The sign-ambiguity item is real — Section 6 states the figure-eight torsion is identified 'up to a sign', following Porti's normalization — and the factor $J_p(\mathscr{E};e^{4N\pi^2/\xi})$ does appear multiplicatively in the winding-$p$ theorems; these are secondary technical aspects of the figure-eight analysis, while the general-knot conjecture is the substantive open core.

Figure-eight lineage: the parametrized conjecture and its relatives are now verified for $4_1$ in essentially all examined regimes — the log-limit for small $|u|$ (Murakami–Yokota, Crelle 2007); the full torsion formula for small $|u|$, $p\ne 0$ (Murakami, J. Topology 2013); the degenerate value $u=\kappa$ (Murakami, AGT 2025, from arXiv:2307.07100); real $u>\kappa$ with $p=0$ (Murakami–Tran, Springer PROMS 2025) and with $p\ge 1$ (arXiv:2312.00350, the audited paper); $\xi=2\pi i$ (Andersen–Hansen 2006); and complex $\xi$ with $0<\operatorname{Im}\xi<\pi/2$ (Murakami, arXiv:2602.01049, February 2026). The introduction of that 2026 paper contains an up-to-date 'known results' list in which every entry concerns the figure-eight knot, confirming the author's own assessment that no other knot is covered.

Other knots: the torsion-level refinement at the unperturbed evaluation $\xi=2\pi i$ is proved for hyperbolic knots with at most seven crossings (Andersen–Hansen, DOI 10.1142/S0218216506004555, for $4_1$; Ohtsuki, DOIs 10.4171/qt/83 and 10.1142/S0129167X17500963, for $5_2$ and the seven-crossing knots; Ohtsuki–Yokota, DOI 10.1017/S0305004117000494, for $6_1,6_2,6_3$), but these are single-point asymptotics without the deformation parameter $u$; they do not verify the parametrized conjecture. No verification of the parametrized conjecture for any hyperbolic knot other than $4_1$ was found in any literature I could search.

Adjacent recent work checked and ruled out as resolution: Jun Murakami (arXiv:2501.00225) proves the ordinary volume conjecture — leading exponential rate only, without the $u$-parameter, torsion, or Chern–Simons prefactor — for double twist knots; Ben Aribi–Guilloux–Wong (arXiv:2512.17437) prove the Andersen–Kashaev volume conjecture, a different conjecture about the colored Jones analytically continued in the color variable, for over 42,000 knot complements via FAMED triangulations; Kakuta (arXiv:2604.10472) studies color-sequence limits for the figure-eight knot and the Borromean rings against cone-manifold volumes; Morozov (arXiv:2606.24497) and Aistleitner–Hauke (arXiv:2510.02188) treat quantum A-polynomial and quantum-modularity aspects. None establishes the parametrized asymptotic formula for a new hyperbolic knot, and none of them claims to.

Coverage and residual uncertainty: retrieval used the arXiv API (phrase searches around 'parametrized volume conjecture', 'colored Jones', 'Reidemeister torsion', 'figure-eight', recent volume-conjecture listings), Crossref, the LKM knowledge graph (bohrium index), and Hitoshi Murakami's complete arXiv bibliography through February 2026. Semantic Scholar citation lookup was persistently rate-limited and OpenAlex was out of budget, so forward-citation chaining was reconstructed from these routes rather than a live citation index; arXiv:2312.00350 itself appears to remain unpublished (no Crossref journal record found as of 2026-08-28). Within this coverage the parametrized volume conjecture for hyperbolic knots beyond the figure-eight knot — including the sign normalization of the homological adjoint Reidemeister torsion, unresolved even for $4_1$ — remains open, with no conflicting evidence.

## Scientific Significance

Affected-field significance: `high`.

Quantum topology and low-dimensional topology. A proof would establish, for every hyperbolic knot, a precise identity between the semiclassical limit of the colored Jones polynomial and the classical geometry — potential function, $\mathrm{PSL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ Chern–Simons invariant, and adjoint Reidemeister torsion — of the deformed $\mathrm{SL}(2;\mathbb{C})$ representations of the knot group. This would directly change core knowledge in the volume-conjecture program: the predicted bridge between quantum invariants and hyperbolic geometry would advance from a single-knot precedent (the figure-eight knot) to a general theorem, confirming at full refinement level what complex Chern–Simons perturbation theory predicts, subsuming the known single-point torsion refinements for knots with at most seven crossings, and settling the torsion sign convention. The impact is direct: it concerns the central open asymptotic structure of the field's flagship invariant rather than an incidental corollary. A refutation would be equally consequential, forcing a revision of the conjectural complex Chern–Simons / quantum-topology dictionary.
