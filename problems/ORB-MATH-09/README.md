# ORB-MATH-09: The motivic t-structure conjecture: existence of a non-degenerate tensor- and realization-compatible t-structure on Voevodsky's category of geometric motives


The standard conjectures of Grothendieck on algebraic cycles - algebraicity of the Kunneth projectors and of the inverse Lefschetz operator on the cohomology of smooth projective varieties - are open in every characteristic. Beilinson (2010) proved that over a field of characteristic zero they follow from the conjectural existence of a motivic t-structure on Voevodsky's triangulated category $DM(k,\mathbb{Q})$ of geometric motives with rational coefficients: a non-degenerate t-structure compatible with the tensor product and with realization functors, whose heart would be the long-conjectured abelian category of mixed motives. The surviving open core is the motivic t-structure conjecture: does such a t-structure exist? The conjecture is open over every base field. Partial results exist only on restricted subcategories of motives (1-motives, Artin motives, Artin-Tate motives), Bondarko's weight structures (a kind of structure dual to a t-structure, known to exist unconditionally) provide a weaker related structure, K-theoretic obstructions constrain possible constructions, and Beilinson also proved that the weak Suslin conjecture on Lawson homology with rational coefficients is equivalent to the standard conjectures themselves. A resolution would either establish the category of mixed motives and the standard conjectures together, or fundamentally reorganize the motivic program.

## Background

Grothendieck's standard conjectures, formulated in the 1960s, are among the central open problems of algebraic geometry. Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over a field $k$, and let $H^\cdot(X)$ be its cohomology in a Weil cohomology theory - for example $\ell$-adic etale cohomology for a prime $\ell$ invertible in $k$, or singular cohomology when $k$ embeds into $\mathbb{C}$. An algebraic correspondence, that is, a cycle class on $X\times X$, acts on $H^\cdot(X)$, and the standard conjectures predict that certain cohomological operators are induced by such correspondences: the Kunneth-type conjecture asserts that the projectors of $H^\cdot(X)$ onto its individual cohomological degrees are algebraic, and the Lefschetz-type conjecture asserts that the inverse of the Lefschetz operator (cup product with the class of a hyperplane section, which is an isomorphism by the hard Lefschetz theorem) is algebraic. Consequences would include the equality of homological and numerical equivalence of cycles and a canonical motivic decomposition of every smooth projective variety. Many special cases are known, but the conjectures are open in general, in every characteristic.

A natural home for these questions is Voevodsky's triangulated category $DM_k=DM(k,\mathbb{Q})$ of geometric motives with rational coefficients: a rigid tensor triangulated category (a tensor triangulated category in which every object has a tensor dual) built from smooth $k$-varieties and algebraic cycles, equipped with the motive functor $M$ and with realization functors $r$ to bounded derived categories of vector spaces - the $\ell$-adic realization for $\ell$ prime to the characteristic of $k$, and, when $k$ has characteristic zero, the Betti realizations attached to embeddings $k\hookrightarrow\mathbb{C}$; the groups $H^a_r(X)=H^a(r(M(X)))$ are the homology theories of $X$ seen through $r$. A t-structure on a triangulated category is a pair of full subcategories, stable under shifts and extensions, that yields truncation functors, cohomology functors, and an abelian heart, generalizing the standard t-structure on a derived category. The motivic t-structure conjecture (stated in Section 1.2 of Beilinson's 2010 paper, with reference to Chapter 21 of Andre's monograph on motives) predicts a t-structure $\mu$ on $DM_k$ that is motivic in Beilinson's sense: non-degenerate (the cohomology functor $\mu H^\cdot$ is conservative, that is, a morphism is an isomorphism as soon as all its cohomology maps are), compatible with the tensor product (the tensor product is t-exact, so the induced duality is t-exact and the heart is a rigid tensor category), and compatible with a realization functor $r$ (that is, $r$ is t-exact). If a motivic t-structure exists, it is unique for given $r$, and over a field of characteristic zero it does not depend on the choice of $r$. Its heart $\mathcal{M}_k$ is then a Tannakian $\mathbb{Q}$-category (a rigid abelian tensor category which, through the exact faithful functor obtained by restricting $r$ to the heart - a fiber functor in the sense of Tannakian theory - becomes equivalent to the category of finite-dimensional representations of an affine group scheme, the motivic Galois group) - the long-conjectured abelian category of mixed motives over $k$, the central missing object of the motivic program.

Beilinson proved that the conjecture has decisive consequences: assuming a motivic t-structure, hard Lefschetz and the primitive decomposition hold at the level of motives, and the standard conjectures of Lefschetz and Kunneth type follow for every smooth projective variety; over a field of characteristic zero, where the remaining standard conjectures reduce to the Lefschetz type (see Chapter 5 of Andre's monograph), all standard conjectures follow. In the same paper Beilinson proved that the weak form of Suslin's conjecture on Lawson homology (a homology theory built from the group completion of the topological monoid of effective algebraic cycles) with rational coefficients is equivalent to the standard conjectures. The motivic t-structure itself, however, is only known on restricted subcategories of motives - for example 1-motives (Deligne's motives generated by algebraic tori and abelian varieties), Artin motives (the motives of zero-dimensional varieties), and Artin-Tate motives over number fields (extensions mixing Artin motives with powers of the Tate motive) - and remains conjectural for the full category $DM(k,\mathbb{Q})$ over every base field, including $k=\mathbb{C}$. A related but weaker structure is known unconditionally: Bondarko's weight structures, a kind of dual of a t-structure (a pair of subcategories yielding weight truncations and a weight heart, in a homotopy-like rather than cohomological sense), exist on $DM(k,\mathbb{Q})$ - the Chow weight structure, whose weight heart is the category of Chow motives, that is, motives of smooth projective varieties built from correspondences modulo rational equivalence - and they partially constrain what a motivic t-structure can be.

## Problem Statement

Let $k$ be a base field and let $DM_k=DM(k,\mathbb{Q})$ be Voevodsky's triangulated category of geometric motives with rational coefficients over $k$, with its tensor product and its realization functors: for a prime $\ell$ invertible in $k$, the $\ell$-adic realization functor, and, when $k$ has characteristic zero, the Betti realization functors attached to embeddings $k\hookrightarrow\mathbb{C}$; these are exact tensor functors to the bounded derived categories of finite-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}_\ell$-vector spaces and $\mathbb{Q}$-vector spaces respectively, each equipped with its standard t-structure. Recall that a t-structure on a triangulated category is a pair of full subcategories, stable under shifts and extensions, that yields truncation functors, cohomology functors, and an abelian heart (as on a derived category), and that an exact functor between triangulated categories equipped with t-structures is t-exact if it carries the heart of the source into the heart of the target. Does there exist a t-structure $\mu$ on $DM_k$ that is motivic in Beilinson's sense, namely such that: (1) $\mu$ is non-degenerate, i.e. the cohomology functor $\mu H^\cdot$ is conservative, so that an object all of whose $\mu$-cohomology objects vanish is zero; (2) $\mu$ is compatible with the tensor product, i.e. the tensor product is t-exact with respect to $\mu$; and (3) $\mu$ is compatible with a realization, i.e. one of the realization functors $r$ above is t-exact with respect to $\mu$ and the standard t-structure on its target?
The problem is to settle this existence question in either direction: construct such a t-structure and verify properties (1)-(3), or prove that no such t-structure exists. The conjecture is open over every base field, including algebraically closed fields of characteristic zero such as $k=\mathbb{C}$. If a motivic t-structure exists it is unique (for given $r$; in characteristic zero independently of the choice of $r$), so the question has a determinate answer. By Beilinson's results, a positive answer over a field of characteristic zero implies Grothendieck's standard conjectures for all smooth projective varieties over that field, and a positive answer over an arbitrary field implies at least the standard conjectures of Lefschetz and Kunneth type there.
