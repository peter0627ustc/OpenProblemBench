# ORB-PHYS-72: Rigorous proof of the universal MPS–Bethe-state overlap formula for orthogonal and symplectic rational spin chains

A universal formula expresses the normalized overlap between an integrable matrix product state (MPS) and an on-shell Bethe eigenstate of a rational (Yangian) spin chain as the product of an MPS-independent ratio of Gaudin determinants and an MPS-dependent prefactor built from eigenvalues of commuting operators constructed from the K-matrix defining the state. Gombor (arXiv:2505.20234, JHEP 2025) proved this formula in full representation-independent generality for $\mathfrak{gl}_N$-symmetric chains using only the algebraic Bethe ansatz and the KT-relation, and proposed its extension to $\mathfrak{so}_N$- and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$-symmetric chains: nested G- and F-operators were defined via algebra embeddings and low-rank isomorphisms, and the one-particle overlap functions were determined at every Bethe node under the assumption that the universal form holds — but the proof was explicitly left open, the author noting that it requires recursion, action and coproduct formulas for the off-shell Bethe vectors of these algebras, then available only for $\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1}$. Those prerequisites have since been established for all classical series (Liashyk–Pakuliak–Ragoucy, 2026), yet no proof of the orthogonal/symplectic universal overlap formula has been published. The problem is to prove — or disprove by an explicit counterexample — the proposed universal overlap formula for $\mathfrak{so}_N$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ rational spin chains in generality matching the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ theorem, including the Bethe-root selection rules and the cases where quasi-determinants of the K-matrix are ill-defined and a deformation-and-limit argument is required.

## Background

Rational spin chains and Bethe states. A length-$L$ quantum spin chain is integrable when its sites carry representations of a symmetry algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ and its transfer matrix is built from an $R$-matrix solving the Yang–Baxter equation. For the rational $\mathfrak{gl}_N$-invariant chain, $R(u)=\mathbf{1}+P/u$ with $P$ the permutation operator; for orthogonal and symplectic chains, $R(u)=\mathbf{1}+P/u-\mathbf{Q}/(u+\kappa_N)$, where $\mathbf{Q}$ is constructed from the invariant bilinear form of $\mathfrak{so}_N$ or $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ ($\mathfrak{sp}_N=\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}$) and $\kappa_N=(N\mp 2)/2$ distinguishes the two cases. The transfer matrices generate the Yangian $Y(\mathfrak{g}_N)$, an infinite-dimensional deformation of the enveloping algebra. In the algebraic Bethe ansatz (ABA), eigenstates (on-shell Bethe states) are created by monodromy-matrix elements acting on a pseudovacuum (the reference state annihilated by the lowering entries of the monodromy matrix) and are labelled by one set of rapidities (Bethe roots) per node of the Dynkin diagram of $\mathfrak{g}_N$; off-shell Bethe vectors are the same operator products evaluated at arbitrary rapidities. The Gaudin matrix is the Jacobian of the Bethe equations with respect to the roots; its determinants give Bethe-state norms and appear inside overlap formulas.

Integrable boundary states and the KT-relation. A matrix product state (MPS) has the form $\langle\Psi|=\sum\mathrm{tr}(A_{i_1}\cdots A_{i_L})\,\langle i_1\cdots i_L|$, where the matrices act on an auxiliary boundary space; a two-site product state is the degenerate case of a one-dimensional boundary space. The MPS is called integrable when its associated K-matrix solves the reflection equation, i.e. furnishes a representation of a twisted Yangian (reflection algebra) — a coideal subalgebra of the Yangian determined by the boundary symmetry. Such overlaps are the central building block of the quench-action method for non-equilibrium integrable dynamics (a saddle-point framework determining post-quench steady states from these overlaps) and of one-point functions in defect versions of $\mathcal{N}=4$ super Yang–Mills theory and of ABJM theory (the Aharony–Bergman–Jafferis–Maldacena Chern–Simons matter theory, the other main integrable instance of the AdS/CFT correspondence). Gombor and Pozsgay (2021) introduced the KT-relation, $\mathbf{K}(u)\,\langle\Psi|T(u)=\langle\Psi|T(-u)\,\mathbf{K}(u)$ with $T(u)$ the monodromy matrix, which reduces overlap computations to purely algebraic Bethe-ansatz manipulations that do not depend on which physical representation the chain sites carry.

The universal overlap formula and its status. Building on ABA overlap proofs for $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ chains (Gombor 2022, 2024) and on the twisted-Yangian construction of MPS overlaps in the SO(6) chain (de Leeuw et al. 2020), Gombor (2024, published in Physical Review Letters 2025) determined the MPS-dependent prefactor for all integrable MPS of the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$, $\mathfrak{o}_N$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ chains without complete proof, and then (Gombor 2025, the source of this problem) proved for $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ the universal formula

$$
\frac{\langle\mathrm{MPS}|\bar{u}\rangle}{\sqrt{\langle\bar{u}|\bar{u}\rangle}}=\Big(\sum_{\ell=1}^{d_B}\beta_{\ell}\prod_{\nu=1}^{n_+}\prod_{k=1}^{r_\nu^+}\tilde{\mathcal{F}}_{\ell}^{(\nu)}(u_k^\nu)\Big)\times\sqrt{\frac{\det G^+}{\det G^-}},
$$

i.e. the normalized overlap factorizes into an MPS-dependent prefactor — eigenvalue products of mutually commuting F-operators built as quasi-determinants (determinant-like combinations $\mathbf{K}_{22}-\mathbf{K}_{21}\mathbf{K}_{11}^{-1}\mathbf{K}_{12}$ of blocks of the nested K-matrices), summed with eigenvalues $\beta_\ell$ of a boundary-space operator — times an MPS-independent ratio of Gaudin determinants fixed by the Bethe equations alone. Section 6 of the same paper then proposes the same universal structure for $\mathfrak{so}_N$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ chains: assuming the universal form, it determines the one-particle overlap functions $\tilde{\mathcal{F}}^{(\nu)}(u)$ at every Bethe node for the reflection algebras $Y(\mathfrak{so}_N,\mathfrak{so}_M\oplus\mathfrak{so}_{N-M})$, $Y(\mathfrak{sp}_{2n},\mathfrak{sp}_{2m}\oplus\mathfrak{sp}_{2n-2m})$ and $Y(\mathfrak{so}_{2n},\mathfrak{gl}_n)$, checks the low-rank cases through the isomorphisms $\mathfrak{sp}_2\cong\mathfrak{gl}_2$, $\mathfrak{so}_3\cong\mathfrak{gl}_2$ (via fusion) and $\mathfrak{so}_4\cong\mathfrak{gl}_2\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_2$, and handles K-matrices with non-invertible blocks by a continuous deformation restoring invertibility, based on Molev's theorem that finite-dimensional irreducible twisted-Yangian representations descend from Yangian ones. No proof is given. The author states that a proof requires recursion, action and coproduct formulas for the off-shell Bethe vectors of $\mathfrak{so}_N$/$\mathfrak{sp}_N$ chains — at the time available only for $\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1}$ (Liashyk–Pakuliak–Ragoucy). Those formulas have since been established for all classical series (Liashyk–Pakuliak–Ragoucy 2026), which makes the missing proof a concrete, well-posed objective.

## Problem Statement

Prove, or disprove by explicit counterexample, the proposed universal overlap formula for $\mathfrak{so}_N$- and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$-symmetric rational spin chains, in the same representation-independent generality in which the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ case is a theorem (Gombor, arXiv:2505.20234). Concretely: for integrable MPS whose K-matrices satisfy the reflection equation and generate the reflection algebras $Y(\mathfrak{so}_N,\mathfrak{so}_M\oplus\mathfrak{so}_{N-M})$, $Y(\mathfrak{sp}_{2n},\mathfrak{sp}_{2m}\oplus\mathfrak{sp}_{2n-2m})$ or $Y(\mathfrak{so}_{2n},\mathfrak{gl}_n)$, and for chains with arbitrary finite-dimensional physical representations, establish that the normalized overlap between the MPS and any on-shell Bethe state equals the product of (i) the MPS-dependent prefactor — the sum over boundary eigenvalues $\beta_\ell$ of products of one-particle overlap functions $\tilde{\mathcal{F}}^{(\nu)}_\ell(u)$, i.e. eigenvalues of the commuting G-/F-operators defined through quasi-determinants of the nested K-matrices — and (ii) the MPS-independent Gaudin-determinant ratio $\sqrt{\det G^+/\det G^-}$. The derivation must also establish the selection rules under which the overlap is non-vanishing — the pair structure of the Bethe roots (roots at each Bethe node occurring in $\pm u$ pairs), including the cases with extra selection rules identified in the source — and must cover the K-matrices whose relevant blocks are non-invertible, where the universal quantities are defined through the deformation-and-limit procedure. The proof should proceed by algebraic-Bethe-ansatz methods — the KT-relation together with recursion, action and coproduct formulas for the off-shell Bethe vectors of the $\mathfrak{g}_N$ chains — matching the rigor and generality of the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ proof. A disproof consists of an explicit integrable $\mathfrak{so}_N$ or $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ MPS and an on-shell Bethe state whose true overlap differs from the proposed formula.

The verification contract below evaluates answers to this statement. It does not narrow or redefine the research question.

Known solving difficulties:

- Adapting the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ proof template (KT-relation, off-shell overlap sum formula, pair-structure limit, on-shell limit) to the $\mathfrak{so}_N$/$\mathfrak{sp}_N$ nesting chains, where the monodromy matrix carries the doubled $\pm i$ index structure, the central element $\gamma(u)=\lambda_{-n}(u)\lambda_n(u-\kappa_N)$ plays a distinguished role, and the crossed and uncrossed KT-relations coincide only through the inversion identity.
- Two structurally different reflection-algebra families ($Y(\mathfrak{g}_N,\mathfrak{g}_M\oplus\mathfrak{g}_{N-M})$ and $Y(\mathfrak{so}_{2n},\mathfrak{gl}_n)$) must be treated, with different nesting chains, and the 'extra selection rule' cases require separate analysis.
- Combining the recently established off-shell Bethe-vector technology for $\mathfrak{g}_N$ chains (recursion, action, coproduct formulas of arXiv:2601.00713) with the nested K-matrix formalism; determinant (Gaudin/norm) representations for $\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}$ and $\mathfrak{o}_{2n}$ Bethe vectors may still have to be derived, since published scalar-product results cover $\mathfrak{o}_{2n+1}$ and $\mathfrak{gl}_n$ only.
- Making the deformation-and-limit argument for non-invertible K-matrix blocks rigorous, including showing that the deformed quasi-determinants have well-defined limits independent of the chosen deformation.
- Establishing commutativity and the eigenvalue structure of the nested G-/F-operators on the boundary space for the general embeddings, beyond the low-rank isomorphism cases $\mathfrak{sp}_2$, $\mathfrak{so}_3$, $\mathfrak{so}_4$ that are checked explicitly.
- Residual risk that the universal form itself fails in some corner (singular K-matrices, representations outside the highest-weight class, or the extra-selection-rule algebras), which would require modifying the formula rather than proving it.

## Current Progress

- Status: `ready`

Source fidelity and scoping (LKM provenance: candidate node gcn_b1fe7c57603e494c). The LKM record bundles four open directions from the Conclusions of Gombor's paper. I verified them against the arXiv HTML (v2) and the published version (JHEP 10 (2025) 035): (i) the proof of the universal overlap formula for orthogonal and symplectic chains, (ii) extension to graded and trigonometric chains, and (iii) the search for a universal formula under twisted boundary conditions are all stated in the Conclusions and are faithfully paraphrased. The fourth LKM bullet, on a 'fully systematic classification' of the deformation method, overstates the source: the paper proves existence of the invertibility-restoring deformation for finite-dimensional irreducible $Y^{-}(2)$ K-matrices via Molev's embedding theorem and does not pose such a classification among its open problems. This record is formulated on item (i), the sharply posed and completion-bounded core — the formula already exists as a proposal with determined one-particle functions, the proof template exists from the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ case, and the author names the exact missing ingredients — while items (ii) and (iii), which also remain open, are documented below so that the reviewer sees the full source picture.

What the source establishes. For $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ chains the universal formula is proven in full representation-independent generality (off-shell overlap sum formula, pair-structure limit, on-shell limit), for all integrable MPS whose K-matrix quasi-determinants exist, with the non-invertible cases treated by a deformation-and-limit argument. The precursor paper (Gombor, arXiv:2410.23282, Phys. Rev. Lett. 2025) had already given, without complete proof, the MPS-dependent prefactor for all integrable MPS of the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$, $\mathfrak{o}_N$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ chains with arbitrary representations. Section 6 of the source paper determines the one-particle overlap functions at each Bethe node for the reflection algebras $Y(\mathfrak{so}_N,\mathfrak{so}_M\oplus\mathfrak{so}_{N-M})$, $Y(\mathfrak{sp}_{2n},\mathfrak{sp}_{2m}\oplus\mathfrak{sp}_{2n-2m})$ and $Y(\mathfrak{so}_{2n},\mathfrak{gl}_n)$, assuming the universal form, and verifies the low-rank isomorphism cases $\mathfrak{sp}_2$, $\mathfrak{so}_3$, $\mathfrak{so}_4$; no proof of the formula itself is provided for any orthogonal or symplectic chain.

The stated missing ingredients have since been supplied. Liashyk–Pakuliak–Ragoucy derived rectangular recurrence relations for $\mathfrak{gl}_n$ and $\mathfrak{o}_{2n+1}$ (arXiv:2412.05224, SIGMA 2025) and scalar products and norms of Bethe vectors for $\mathfrak{o}_{2n+1}$ (arXiv:2503.01578, SciPost Phys. 2025) — the ingredients Gombor noted as available for $\mathfrak{so}_{2n+1}$ at the time. In January 2026 the same authors (arXiv:2601.00713) gave a precise definition of off-shell Bethe vectors for $\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{gl}_n$, $\mathfrak{o}_{2n+1}$, $\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}$ and $\mathfrak{o}_{2n}$ and proved from it the action formulas of monodromy entries, the rectangular recurrence relations and the coproduct formula; the Yangian-double construction of these vectors followed in arXiv:2607.00594. These are exactly the prerequisites Gombor identified ('with these formulas in hand, the proofs in the current paper could likely be applied without difficulty'). However, as of late August 2026 nobody has combined them with the Section 6 proposal into a proof: arXiv:2601.00713 has only two citations (the authors' own sequel and an unrelated work), and none of the works citing the source paper proves the orthogonal/symplectic formula.

Partial case result. Gombor and Holguin (arXiv:2510.27463, Phys. Lett. B 2026) proved the integrability of $\mathfrak{so}_4$-invariant fuzzy-three-sphere MPS in the $\mathfrak{so}_6$-symmetric chain, exhibited the resulting boundary reflection algebra for every representation of the fuzzy three sphere, and derived closed overlap formulas with Bethe states for arbitrary bond dimension. This is a single reflection-algebra family (and $\mathfrak{so}_6\cong\mathfrak{sl}_4$ places it close to the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ framework), not the general universal formula, and its authors do not claim the general proof.

Status of the source's other two open directions (documented, not part of this problem's core). Twisted boundaries: Gombor–Kristjansen–Moustakis–Qian (arXiv:2410.23117, JHEP 2025) derived summation and dressing formulas valid with inhomogeneities and twists, specialized to SO(6); Ekhammar–Gromov–Ryan (arXiv:2312.11612, JHEP 2024) obtained boundary overlaps for twisted chains from functional separation of variables, where the determinant involves Q-functions rather than Gaudin determinants; Qian–Zhang (arXiv:2601.16605, Jan 2026) constructed factorized integrable boundary states of XXZ and XYZ chains under periodic and twisted boundary conditions (both parities of system length) via KT-relations, derived their selection rules, and computed overlaps in the U(1)-symmetric cases — progress on the trigonometric and twist fronts, but for product-type states and case by case; no universal operator-level MPS formula under twist is known. Graded chains: recent ABJM-chain works constructed chiral integrable boundary states and closed-form overlaps for the alternating SU(4) chain (arXiv:2602.01697; arXiv:2607.04203), and Kristjansen–Shusharin (arXiv:2608.10840) showed that their overlap formula extends covariantly under fermionic dualities toward the graded $\mathfrak{osp}(4|6)$ Dynkin diagram — evidence toward, but far from, a universal graded formula.

Coverage and uncertainty. The audit used the Semantic Scholar citation graph (7 citations of the source) and Inspire-HEP (8 citing records, current through mid-August 2026), an Inspire author listing of all of Gombor's works (no sequel proving the so/sp case), and arXiv keyword sweeps of the MPS-overlap corpus in hep-th; all routes agree that the orthogonal/symplectic proof is unpublished. Zhang–Wu–Cheng–Tu (arXiv:2508.13114) used exact overlap formulas for SO(n)-symmetric AKLT (Affleck–Kennedy–Lieb–Tasaki) states in SU(n) chains — an application within the $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ family, not the $\mathfrak{so}_N$-chain problem. Remaining uncertainty: citation indices lag by weeks, so a very recent proof preprint could be missed; a proof could also be embedded in a thesis or monograph not indexed as citing the source; and determinant (norm/Gaudin) representations for $\mathfrak{sp}_{2n}$ and $\mathfrak{o}_{2n}$ Bethe vectors, which a full proof may additionally need, are not pinned down by this audit.

Open core. A rigorous, representation-independent proof (or refutation) of the proposed universal overlap formula for $\mathfrak{so}_N$ and $\mathfrak{sp}_N$ rational spin chains is absent from the literature, and the problem is now concrete: the Bethe-vector prerequisites named by the source were completed in 2026, the proposal with its one-particle functions is published, and partial case results (low-rank isomorphisms; the fuzzy-three-sphere family) support but do not establish it. The outcome is therefore open.

## Scientific Significance

Affected-field significance: `medium`.

Solving the problem would directly extend the rigorously proven scope of the universal MPS–Bethe-state overlap formula from $\mathfrak{gl}_N$ to the full classical series of rational spin chains: researchers would gain exact, representation-independent overlap formulas (with their selection rules) for orthogonal and symplectic chains, which currently exist only as an unproven proposal supported by low-rank checks. This is a direct capability gain for quench-action studies of non-equilibrium dynamics in higher-rank chains and for defect one-point-function computations in AdS/CFT with orthogonal or symplectic symmetry, and it would supply the template on which the further extensions the source poses (graded, trigonometric, twisted) would presumably be built. The impact is substantive but confined to the integrable-spin-chain and integrable-boundary-state community rather than changing the broader field's core methods, so it is rated medium.
