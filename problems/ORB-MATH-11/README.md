# ORB-MATH-11: The Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture for Artin Algebras

The Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture (GSC), recorded by Auslander–Reiten–Smalø, predicts that for any artin algebra $\Lambda$, finiteness of the self-injective dimension on one side implies finiteness on the other: if $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda<\infty$ then $\mathrm{id}\,\Lambda_{\Lambda}<\infty$. Huang (2022) proved, via his equivalence theorem for relative homological dimensions over left and right Noetherian rings, that GSC is equivalent to a family of module-level statements: for every artin algebra with $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda\le n$, the Gorenstein projective (equivalently Gorenstein injective, Gorenstein flat, strongly Gorenstein flat, or projectively coresolved Gorenstein flat) dimension of every module is uniformly bounded by $n$ on at least one side. Despite substantial partial results (weakly Gorenstein and monomial algebras, reductions via recollements and excellent extensions, Auslander-type conditions) and one claimed proof withdrawn by its author in July 2026, the conjecture remains open for arbitrary artin algebras. This audit verified the source attribution, reconstructed the equivalence, and confirmed by forward-citation and exact-phrase searches that no resolution or counterexample is known.

## Background

An artin algebra is a ring $\Lambda$ that is finitely generated as a module over its center, which is a commutative artinian ring; the model case is a finite-dimensional algebra over a field, typically given by a quiver with relations — a directed graph whose paths, taken modulo the imposed relations, form a basis of the algebra. The left and right finitely generated module categories of $\Lambda$ are linked by a standard duality, but homological properties of $\Lambda$ as a left versus right module over itself need not coincide. The injective dimension $\mathrm{id}\,M$ of a module is the minimal length of an injective resolution of $M$, and the self-injective dimensions of $\Lambda$ are $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda$ and $\mathrm{id}\,\Lambda_{\Lambda}$. The algebra is called Gorenstein (or Iwanaga–Gorenstein) when both are finite, and $n$-Gorenstein when both are at most $n$; a classical lemma of Auslander–Reiten shows that when both self-injective dimensions are finite they are equal. Gorenstein algebras are the natural home of Gorenstein homological algebra, where the role of projectives is played by Gorenstein projective modules — modules appearing as cycles of a totally acyclic complex of projective modules, i.e. an acyclic complex of projectives that stays acyclic after applying $\mathrm{Hom}(-,P)$ for every projective $P$ — dually for Gorenstein injective modules, and analogously for Gorenstein flat modules (cycles of acyclic complexes of flat modules staying acyclic after tensoring with any injective), strongly Gorenstein flat modules (cycles of acyclic complexes of projectives staying acyclic after tensoring with any injective, in the sense of Ding–Li–Mao), and projectively coresolved Gorenstein flat modules (cycles of acyclic complexes of projectives staying acyclic after applying $\mathrm{Hom}(-,F)$ for every flat $F$). Each class yields a relative dimension of an arbitrary module, namely the minimal length of a resolution by objects of the class. The Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture, recorded on p. 410 of Auslander–Reiten–Smalø's book 'Representation Theory of Artin Algebras' (1995), states that for any artin algebra $\Lambda$, if $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda<\infty$ then $\mathrm{id}\,\Lambda_{\Lambda}<\infty$. It is one of the long-standing homological conjectures for artin algebras, studied alongside the Nakayama conjecture — which predicts that every non-self-injective artin algebra has finite dominant dimension, meaning only finitely many leading terms in a minimal injective resolution of the regular module are projective — and it underlies whether 'Gorensteinness' of an artin algebra can be detected from a single side. Huang's paper 'Homological dimensions relative to preresolving subcategories II' proves that for a left and right Noetherian ring $R$, being $n$-Gorenstein is equivalent to each of the one-sided conditions that the Gorenstein projective (equivalently Gorenstein injective, Gorenstein flat, strongly Gorenstein flat, or projectively coresolved Gorenstein flat) dimension of every left $R$-module is at most $n$ (Theorem 1.2 = Theorems 4.9, 4.11, 4.13), and observes as a consequence that GSC is equivalent to these module-level uniform-bound implications for artin algebras of finite one-sided self-injective dimension — the formulation carried by the candidate record under audit.

## Problem Statement

Prove or refute the Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture: for every artin algebra $\Lambda$, if the injective dimension of $\Lambda$ as a left module over itself is finite, $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda<\infty$, then the injective dimension of $\Lambda$ as a right module over itself is also finite, $\mathrm{id}\,\Lambda_{\Lambda}<\infty$ (by symmetry the same implication with left and right interchanged is part of the conjecture; when both sides are finite they are known to be equal). Equivalently, by Huang's Theorem 1.2 (arXiv:2112.12977), establish or refute the following module-level statement: for every artin algebra $\Lambda$ and every $n\ge 0$ with $\mathrm{id}\,{}_{\Lambda}\Lambda\le n$, the Gorenstein projective dimension of every left $\Lambda$-module is at most $n$ — equivalently, the Gorenstein injective, the Gorenstein flat, the strongly Gorenstein flat, or the projectively coresolved Gorenstein flat dimension of every left $\Lambda$-module is at most $n$, or the corresponding statement holds for right modules over $\Lambda^{\mathrm{op}}$. A refutation consists of an artin algebra with finite self-injective dimension on exactly one side, which would also falsify every equivalent module-level implication.

The verification contract below evaluates answers to this statement. It does not narrow or redefine the research question.

Known solving difficulties:

- The equivalence machinery of Huang reduces the conjecture to module-level uniform bounds on Gorenstein-type dimensions, but deriving any such bound from one-sided finiteness of the self-injective dimension for an arbitrary artin algebra is exactly the open content, and no available technique reaches it.
- All known positive cases depend on additional structure — weak Gorensteinness, Auslander-type conditions, monomial or representation-finite type, commutativity — and these arguments break down for arbitrary artin algebras.
- A counterexample, if one exists, would be a finite-dimensional algebra with finite self-injective dimension on one side and infinite on the other; constructing one and rigorously certifying the infinite side appears difficult, and no candidate algebra is known in the literature.
- Derived-category reductions (to 2-derived-simple algebras, or transfers along excellent extensions) shrink the class of algebras to be checked but do not currently decide the conjecture.

## Current Progress

- Status: `ready`

Source fidelity: I downloaded and read the source paper (Huang, arXiv:2112.12977 = Forum Mathematicum 34 (2022), no. 2, 507–530, DOI 10.1515/forum-2021-0136). Its introduction states the Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture exactly as the candidate records it (citing Auslander–Reiten–Smalø's book, p. 410), and states verbatim that by Theorem 1.2 the conjecture is equivalent to: for any artin algebra R with left self-injective dimension at most n, one (hence any) of the module-level conditions (2)–(6), or their opposite-side versions, is satisfied — the conditions being the uniform bound n on Gorenstein projective, Gorenstein injective, Gorenstein flat, strongly Gorenstein flat, or projectively coresolved Gorenstein flat dimension of all modules. The LKM paraphrase is source-faithful; the underlying open problem is the Gorenstein Symmetric Conjecture itself, with Huang's equivalences as derived equivalent formulations, and the record is formulated accordingly.

Partial results: Marczinzik (arXiv:1608.04212, Proposition 3.9(2), which incorporates arXiv:1908.04738) proved that GSC holds for left weakly Gorenstein algebras — algebras over which every semi-Gorenstein-projective module (a cycle of an acyclic complex of projectives acyclic after $\mathrm{Hom}(-,\Lambda)$) is Gorenstein projective; since monomial algebras and endomorphism algebras of modules over representation-finite algebras are left weakly Gorenstein (building on Ringel–Zhang, Algebra & Number Theory 14 (2020)), GSC holds for these classes; commutative artin algebras satisfy it trivially. These are the principal published positive cases beyond finite global dimension, and none extends to arbitrary artin algebras.

Reductions and conditional progress: Han–Qin (Algebras and Representation Theory 19 (2016), no. 2, 377–395; arXiv:1410.3223) reduced GSC to 2-derived-simple algebras using n-recollements (exact sequences of triangulated categories) of derived categories; Yingying Zhang (Algebra Colloquium 25 (2018), no. 4, 619–626; arXiv:1702.05902) showed GSC transfers along excellent extensions of rings; Huang (Bull. London Math. Soc. 56 (2024), no. 11, 3382–3399; arXiv:2408.05468) proved that under Auslander-type conditions an artin algebra is Gorenstein if and only if it is weakly Gorenstein, giving conditional support for the adjacent Auslander–Reiten conjecture; Shaul (arXiv:2303.08756, with an appendix by Nakamura–Thompson) gave several new characterizations of noetherian rings that satisfy GSC in terms of acyclic complexes of injectives and finitistic dimensions — a characterization, not a resolution. Huang–Zhang (J. Algebra 321 (2009), no. 10, 2829–2842; arXiv:0903.0758) earlier connected GSC to the existence of maximal n-orthogonal subcategories for cotilting modules. Chen's survey (arXiv:1712.04587) documents the conjecture's central, unresolved role in the Gorenstein homological algebra of artin algebras.

Claimed resolution check: the only claimed general proof located in a systematic sweep is Weiqing Li, arXiv:2607.28011, "On a proof of the Gorenstein Symmetry Conjecture" (v1 posted 30 July 2026). The author withdrew it the next day (v2, 31 July 2026) with the comment "Since there is an error in the proof, I hereby withdraw the article". The only other recent claim located (reviewer's exact-phrase arXiv recency search, August 2026) is Becerril (arXiv:2602.00932), which confirms GSC only under two specific additional situations on the ring and is likewise a partial result. Neither provides evidence of resolution, and no counterexample to GSC appears in any source inspected.

Forward-citation chain: via the Semantic Scholar Graph API I enumerated all located citing works of the source paper (2022–2026: Huang's own follow-ups on Foxby equivalence and generalized Gorenstein categories; El Maaouy on relative PGF dimensions; Estrada–Iacob–Wang; Duarte–García Rozas–Ouberka–Oyonarte; Liu–Wu–Wang; Li–Huang; Wang–Li–Wu–Hu; Liu–Wei; Fan–Tang). All develop the relative and semidualizing-bimodule machinery; none establishes or refutes the module-level uniform-bound implications for artin algebras of finite one-sided self-injective dimension, and none resolves GSC.

Auxiliary lead from the source: Huang's Remark 4.4(8) notes only conditionally that if the class $GF_C(R)$ of $C$-Gorenstein flat modules (for a semidualizing bimodule $_RC_S$, a bimodule finitely generated and self-orthogonal on both sides serving as a relative duality agent) is closed under extensions, then it is resolving; the unconditional extension-closure without coherence-type or semi-definability hypotheses is not established in the source, and in the citing literature I inspected it is circumvented by such hypotheses rather than settled. This is recorded as auxiliary machinery, not folded into the main problem.

Coverage and residual uncertainty: evidence routes were exact-phrase and title searches on arXiv ("Gorenstein symmetric conjecture", "Gorenstein symmetry conjecture", "Gorenstein symmetry", weakly Gorenstein, Ringel author search), forward citations of the source paper, Crossref metadata checks, and the surveys above. Journal-only literature not mirrored on arXiv was not exhaustively swept, so further partial results may exist, but no claimed general resolution or counterexample surfaced on any route. The open core — GSC for arbitrary artin algebras, equivalently the module-level uniform-bound implications above — is precise and, on the evidence assembled, unresolved as of August 2026.

## Scientific Significance

Affected-field significance: `high`.

Direct impact on the core knowledge of the representation theory of algebras and Gorenstein homological algebra. A proof would make the Gorenstein property of an artin algebra detectable from a single side — a foundational structural fact assumed throughout the theory of Gorenstein algebras, Gorenstein projective/injective modules, singularity categories, and CM-finite algebras — and would settle a conjecture open since Auslander–Reiten–Smalø's 1995 monograph, unlocking the module-level uniform bound equivalences of Huang's Theorem 1.2 as working tools. A counterexample would equally restructure the field by exhibiting genuinely one-sided Gorenstein artin algebras, forcing the two-sided hypotheses currently used in relative homological algebra to be taken seriously as restrictions.
